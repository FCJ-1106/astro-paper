**Black stool, often called "tarry stool," can be a sign of gastrointestinal bleeding**, which occurs when blood breaks down in the intestines, causing the stool to appear black.

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/af/77/cRzQjkxm_o.png" alt="Gastrointestinal-bleeding-Emoticon-package" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
  <figcaption style="text-align: center;">▲ Source: Tencent Medical Dictionary Gastrointestinal bleeding</figcaption>
</figure>

In addition to gastrointestinal bleeding, black stools may also be caused by taking certain medications or foods:

**- Medicines: such as iron supplements and bismuth-containing stomach medicines**<br><br>
**- Food: large amounts of animal liver, blood products, caramel, etc.**

In other words, the "blackening" of feces may be a big mistake made by ourselves.

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/03/b6/Kk6ksiRn_o.png" alt="poop-color-Classification-Emoticon-package" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
  <figcaption style="text-align: center;">▲ Source: Dingxiang Doctor</figcaption>
</figure>

What we want to remind everyone is that if you find that your stool is black and **accompanied by other symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, dizziness , etc.**, it is recommended that you go to the hospital as soon as possible to determine the specific cause and location of the bleeding.

Especially for upper gastrointestinal bleeding, such as **peptic ulcer, rupture of esophageal varices , etc.**, don't delay, go see a doctor!
