While beer contains calories, it's usually not enough on its own to directly cause belly fat accumulation.

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/86/63/oNm8rYt2_o.jpg" alt="The truth about beer belly" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
  <figcaption style="text-align: center;">▲ Source: Tencent Medical Dictionary</figcaption>
</figure>

The beer belly is caused by **lifestyle and eating habits**, which make the energy intake greater than the energy consumption, leading to **"abdominal obesity".**

People who drink beer regularly often pair it with high-calorie foods, such as **barbecue, fried chicken, French fries , etc.**, which makes them more likely to suffer from abdominal obesity.

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/39/03/MMY69YaY_o.gif" alt="Beer BBQ gif" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
  <figcaption style="text-align: center;">▲ Source: Guangzhou Sunyeyi Street</figcaption>
</figure>

In addition, the products of alcohol metabolism will delay fat burning, promote fat synthesis, and make fat more easily stored. Alcohol may also further aggravate abdominal fat accumulation by affecting **hormone levels.**

**Genetics** also play a role, with some people more likely to accumulate fat around their abdomens; **bad lifestyle habits such as lack of exercise and prolonged sitting** can also exacerbate the problem.

</figure>

If you want to get rid of your beer belly, don’t follow the young people’s way of staying up late. **Sufficient sleep** is the key to getting rid of your beer belly.
