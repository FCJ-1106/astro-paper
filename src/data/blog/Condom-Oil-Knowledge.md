## 01 <br>What is the “oil” on condoms?

The layer of liquid on the condom is actually **lubricant**. Don't underestimate this little bit of lubricant, it can reduce the friction coefficient, thereby reducing pain and improving the sexual experience. It is an indispensable component of condoms.

There are three types of lubricants: **water-based, silicone-based, and oil-based.**

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/43/ce/eEiImeYC_o.jpg" alt="three types of lubricants" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
  <figcaption style="text-align: center;">▲ Source: Curious Doctor</figcaption>
</figure>

### 1. Water-based lubricant

The most common lubricant on the market is water-based lubricant. The main ingredients are glycerin, water, small molecule moisturizers, thickeners<sub>(cellulose ethers, carbomer)</sub> , hyaluronic acid, etc.

Its texture is closest to water, has **a good skin-friendly experience, and is easier to clean.**

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/26/52/3yUMoKTn_o.png" alt="Water-based lubricant picture" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
  <figcaption style="text-align: center;">▲ Source: Curious Doctor</figcaption>
</figure>

The disadvantages are that it is easily **absorbed and evaporated, has a short lubrication time**, and will become dry over time.

In addition, studies have shown that lubricants formulated with high concentrations of glycerin, propylene glycol, polyquaternium compounds or other ingredients can easily weaken the barrier function of vaginal epithelial cells, thereby **easily causing inflammation and other problems.**

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/bb/f7/wcvnaKfr_o.png" alt="Three types of inflammation" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
  <figcaption style="text-align: center;">▲ Source: Taishuai Gallery</figcaption>
</figure>

To put it bluntly, when buying water-based lubricants, you should choose products that are a little thinner, not too thick, and it is best **if water is listed first in the ingredient list! !**

### 2. Silicone-based lubricant

Silicone-based lubricants are generally **silicone oil**, which is the most widely used lubricant in natural latex condoms. **The layer of "oil" that usually comes on condoms is silicone oil.**

Unlike oil-based lubricants, which will not damage latex condoms, it **has an oil-like texture and can provide a longer-lasting lubrication experience.**

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/8b/b8/b5aUrgFJ_o.png" alt="Silicone-based lubricants for latex condoms" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
</figure>

The disadvantage is that it is not as easy to wash off as water-based lubricants and cannot be used on silicone sex toys.

Studies have found that whether or not to wear a condom has no significant effect on the vaginal or cervical epithelium, **and that eight hours after sexual intercourse, there is no change in vaginal pH, vaginal lactobacilli, or other normal vaginal flora.**

### 3. Oil-based lubricants

Oil-based lubricants are generally products made from mineral oil, and are not limited to animal and vegetable oils and synthetic hydrocarbons. For example, some raw mineral baby oils belong to this category.

However, oil-based lubricants are rarely seen on the market, mainly because they will **dissolve natural latex condoms , increase the risk of condom rupture, and greatly reduce the contraceptive effect.**

## 02 <br>Will the oil on the condom be harmful to a girl’s private parts?
