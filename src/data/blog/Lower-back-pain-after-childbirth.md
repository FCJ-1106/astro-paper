Painless childbirth uses **spinal anesthesia** technology.

The drug only acts on local nerves, **does not enter the mother's blood circulation, and has no effect on the fetus.**

<figure>
  <img src="https://images2.imgbox.com/3c/c7/n2jnugLq_o.gif" alt="Spinal anesthesia gif" style="width: 100%; height: auto;">
  <figcaption style="text-align: center;">▲ Source: Taishuai Gallery(Spinal anesthesia)</figcaption>
</figure>

Blocking pain signals through spinal anesthesia can reduce the pain of parturients from "level 10 severe pain" to "tolerable soreness and swelling";

In addition, painless childbirth can preserve the mother's feeling of uterine contractions and ability to move, so she doesn't have to worry about not being able to exert strength at critical moments.
