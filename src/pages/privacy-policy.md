Welcome to *NHC Health Science*! We’re all about making health science fun and accessible, and we’re just as serious about keeping your personal info safe. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and (spoiler alert) don’t misuse your data. Let’s dive in!


### **1. What Information We Collect**

We’re not nosy, promise! We only collect what we need to make your experience better. Here’s what we might gather:

- **Stuff You Give Us Directly**: If you fill out our contact form or send us an [email](mailto:fcj1106@gmail.com), we’ll collect things like your name, email address, and whatever message you send. We might also ask for optional info in future tools (like age or weight for calculators), but that’s totally up to you.
  
- **Stuff We Collect Automatically**: Like most websites, we use cookies and analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) to track basic things like your browser type, IP address, and which pages you visit. It’s all anonymous and helps us see what you like (or don’t).

- **Stuff We Don’t Collect**: We don’t ask for sensitive info like your Social Security number, credit card details, or your secret cookie recipe. If you’re not handing it over, we’re not taking it.


### **2. How We Use Your Information**

We’re not selling your data to sketchy third parties or building a creepy profile on you. Here’s what we do with it:

- **To Respond to You**: If you email us or fill out a form, we’ll use your info to reply—like sending you a “Hey, thanks for the suggestion!” or answering your question about why coffee makes you jittery.
  
- **To Improve the Site**: Those anonymous analytics help us figure out which articles are a hit and which ones flop. It’s like a virtual thumbs-up or thumbs-down from you.

- **For Future Features**: If we roll out tools (like a water intake calculator), we might use your inputs to generate results—but only on your device, not stored by us unless you explicitly opt in.


### **3. How We Protect Your Information**

We treat your data like it’s our grandma’s secret recipe—locked up tight. Here’s how we keep it safe:

- **Secure Hosting**: Our website runs on secure servers with encryption (HTTPS) to keep snoops out.
  
- **Limited Access**: Only our tiny team can access your info (like emails), and even then, it’s just for replying to you.

- **No Long-Term Storage**: If you send us a message, we don’t hoard your details forever. Once we’ve responded, we’ll delete it unless there’s a reason to keep it (like ongoing chats).


### **4. Cookies & Tracking**

Yeah, we use cookies—but not the chocolate chip kind. These are little bits of data that help our site work better:

- **Why We Use Them**: Cookies let us remember your preferences (like dark mode) and track site usage (anonymously) so we can improve stuff.

- **Opting Out**: Don’t like cookies? You can disable them in your browser settings. No hard feelings! Just note some features might not work as smoothly.

- **Third-Party Tools**: We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit our site and what they’re reading. They anonymize your data before we see it—check their policy [here](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more.


### **5. Sharing Your Information**

We don’t share your personal info with anyone, unless:

- **You Say It’s Cool**: If you ask us to share something (like submitting a guest post), we’ll do it with your consent.

- **Legal Stuff**: If the law knocks on our door (like a court order), we’d have to comply—but we’ll fight tooth and nail to protect you first.

- **Service Providers**: We might use third-party services (like email platforms) to send messages, but they’re contractually sworn to secrecy.


### **6. Your Choices & Rights**

You’ve got control over your data. Here’s what you can do:

- **Access or Update**: Wanna see what info we have on you? Just ask! (Spoiler: it’s probably just your email if you contacted us.) Email us at **fcj1106@gmail.com**.

- **Delete**: Want us to erase your data? Say the word, and it’s gone—unless we legally need to keep it (rare).

- **Opt-Out**: If we ever send emails (like a newsletter), there’s an unsubscribe link faster than you can say “unsubscribe.”


### **7. Children’s Privacy**

We love kids (they’re hilarious!), but our site isn’t designed for anyone under 13. If we accidentally collect info from a child, let us know, and we’ll delete it faster than you can say “screen time limit.”


### **8. Changes to This Policy**

We might tweak this policy as the site grows (like when we add those fancy tools). If we make big changes, we’ll post a notice on the site or email you (if we have your address). Check back here for updates—last update was March 22, 2025.
